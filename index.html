Echo State Networks

Learning Unidirectional and Bidirectional Coupling of Discrete Maps

Built and benchmarked Echo State Networks to learn unidirectional and bidirectional coupling rules between chaotic dynamical systems (Hénon, Lorenz, Mira, Nordmark, Lozi maps), demonstrating that reservoir models trained on one drive-response pair could generalize to entirely unseen drive signals. Designed and evaluated 9 custom reservoir network topologies (Lattice, Ring, Star, Random, Scale-Free, Small-World, Mesh, and hybrids), finding that densely-connected topologies like Random and Mesh consistently outperformed hub-based structures across all tested systems, with performance validated using RMSE, MAE, and KL divergence.
